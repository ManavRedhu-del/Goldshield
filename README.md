# 🛡️ GoldShield
**Every gram accounted for. No more disputes.**

A free, bilingual (**English + Hindi**) web application designed to protect customers, shopkeepers, and artisans from hidden charges, purity doubts, and gold loss across the jewellery supply chain.

---

## 🚨 The Problem
Buying, selling, and crafting gold jewellery in India still relies heavily on verbal promises, leading to daily financial losses and disputes:
* **Hidden Charges:** Handwritten *"kacha"* slips contain unexplained wastage and making charges, making it hard for customers to know if they are being overcharged.
* **Purity Doubts:** Buyers struggle to verify whether the physical hallmark matches the piece and the final bill.
* **Unknown Melt Loss:** When old gold is melted and remade, there is no transparent way to track how much pure gold was genuinely lost.
* **Blind Ordering:** Customers cannot visualize how design modifications change gold weight and final pricing before placing an order.
* **Artisan Accountability:** Shopkeepers lack simple tools to track gold issued to artisans or evaluate workshop wastage fairly.
* **Language & Tech Barriers:** Existing jewellery ERPs are complex, expensive, and English-only.

---

## 💡 Our Solution: One App, Three Roles
GoldShield unites **Customers**, **Shopkeepers**, and **Artisans** on a single, zero-signup platform where everyone operates on the same transparent math.

### How It Works
1. **Select Your Role & Rate:** Choose Customer, Shopkeeper, or Artisan and enter today's gold rate once.
2. **Verify or Track:** Run a price audit, purity check, 3D customization, or workshop log. Every tool outputs a plain-language **Green, Amber, or Red verdict**.
3. **Act with Confidence:** See exact rupee differences and actionable next steps before paying or handing over gold.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👤 For Customers
* **Price Breakdown:** Splits any quote into pure gold value, wastage, making charges, and GST, flagging rates above standard market ranges.
* **Handwritten Quote (*Kacha* Slip) Checker:** Audits rough shop estimates to reveal exact extra charges.
* **Is It Real Gold? (Hallmark & HUID Guide):** Explains BIS hallmarks, guides users to verify HUIDs via the official BIS Care app, and cross-checks stamped purity against the bill.
* **Water Density Test:** Estimates gold purity using two simple weighings *(clearly marked as an indicative check)*.
* **Old Gold Exchange Calculator:** Calculates true scrap value and flags unfair shop deductions.
* **Melt & Remake Check:** Compares actual gold loss against the standard 0.5%–2% industry benchmark.
* **Provenance Check:** Assigns a Green, Amber, or Red status for legal traceability.

### 🏪 For Shopkeepers & Artisans
* **Artisan Job Tracker:** Records gold issued for crafting, checks wastage upon return, and flags unusual discrepancies.
* **Artisan Scorecard:** Automatically categorizes artisans as *New*, *Reliable*, or *Watch* based on historical average loss.
* **Workbench Ledger:** Tracks gold weight across crafting stages with **hands-free voice input**.
* **Gold-Print:** Captures the unique acoustic tap-sound signature of a gold piece for physical verification.

### 🌐 For Everyone
* **Interactive 3D Ornament Designer:** Customize ornament shapes, patterns, and gemstones in 3D while watching estimated gold weight and pricing update in real time.
* **Zero-Barrier Accessibility:** Full **Hindi & English** interface with voice explanations, plus Light and Dark modes.
